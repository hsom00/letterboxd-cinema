Welcome to your cinema

In a few minutes this becomes a place to watch the films on your Letterboxd watchlist — found, fetched and shelved for you, and shown off properly.

Four short questions. Nothing you can't change later.

What's it called?

This goes on the marquee at the top of every page. Your surname, your street, your cat — whatever a cinema of yours would be called.

Your account

You'll sign in with this on every device. It's also the key to the projection booth, so make the password a real one.

Your Letterboxd

Add a film to your Letterboxd watchlist and it turns up here on its own, usually within the hour. This is the whole point — but it's optional, and you can add it later in Settings.

Your watchlist needs to be public on Letterboxd (it is by default).

Where films come from

The cinema searches public sources for the films you want. Pick a few — three or four is plenty — or skip this and add sources in Settings whenever you like.

Setting up

Creating your account, building the library, and wiring the machinery together. About a minute.

    Your cinema is ready

    Sign in with the account you just made. Films from your watchlist will start arriving shortly — the first few take a while, because they're being found and fetched from scratch.

    Anything else — sources, your Letterboxd, the name — lives under your initial in the top-right corner, in Settings.
